Research

Older adults are the fastest-growing group left out of transportation planning

My master's thesis examined how the demographic and travel characteristics of older adults in Tennessee changed between 2009 and 2022, and what that means for future planning.

Demographic and Travel Characteristics of Older Adults in Tennessee

Built eight trip generation models, linear regression, Poisson, negative binomial, ZIP, ZINB, hurdle NB, ordered logit, and ordered probit, using 2009 and 2022 NHTS Tennessee add-on data.

Tested how well models built in one year or region transferred to another, using Transfer Index and Transfer R² methods, then ranked every model by weighted mean absolute error.

  • Weighted mean trip rates fell from 2.91 in 2009 to 1.86 in 2022.
  • Auto mobility was the dominant predictor of trip-making in both years.
  • Linear regression ranked first for accuracy in 2009. ZINB ranked first in 2022.
  • Models transferred poorly across time, and spatial transferability declined from 2009 to 2022.
